An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) Growth bounty
**"Sum up 19 November's world news in one image"**
(`news-image-20261119`, $250 in prizes).

## The image

`entry.png` is a 1024×1024 newspaper front page, **THE WORLD TODAY** for
Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn for this entry:

- **Lead:** Gamers enter Vice City as GTA VI launches worldwide — after a
  13-year wait, Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI arrives with enormous hype.
- **U.S. politics:** Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker of the
  House, in line to become the first Black Speaker.
- **Asia diplomacy:** Trump arrives in China for talks with Xi Jinping and
  Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.
- **Europe:** the EU proposes a 45 billion euro loan for Kyiv, to be repaid
  after Russia pays reparations.
- **In brief:** Pew on Islam by 2035, Cyclone Akvan, Voyager 1's light-day
  milestone, Nvidia's doubled revenue, Hegseth in Venezuela, and India
  winning the SAFF Championship final.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte for byte. Running it twice writes
the same bytes both times; no network is used.

## Reproduce

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
# writes /tmp/out/entry.png, identical to entry.png
```
